Our mission

Make organic waste a local resource.

↗

The challenge

A missed opportunity hiding in plain sight.

Fresh produce markets generate organic leftovers every day. Without a coordinated local collection system, valuable material can be mixed with general waste and sent to landfill.

01

Fragmented collection

Vendors need a dependable, simple way to schedule organic waste pickups.

02

Uncoordinated transport

Local drivers need clear pickup requests and a destination they can plan around.

03

Lost resource value

Segregated organic waste can become a useful input for local fertilizer production.

How it works

A short journey from stall to soil.

Green-Loop coordinates the people and places that make localized reverse logistics possible.

1

Vendor logs waste

Record the weight and request a pickup.

2

Driver collects

Accept a nearby request and transport the load.

3

Centre processes

Organic waste is converted into fertilizer.

4

Value returns

Fertilizer is sold into the agricultural market.
